## THE NAME

**ปล่อยของ — Ploikong**

Three meanings living in the same word:

**Let it go** — the collector's hardest moment. You spent years finding it, learning it, loving it. Now you release it to someone who will continue that love. This is not selling. This is passing something forward.

**Release your power** — in Thai slang, ปล่อยของ means a master releasing their knowledge, their trick, their expertise. When an expert collector puts something on Ploikong, they are not just listing an item. They are releasing years of taste, research, and hard-won insider knowledge into the world.

**The spiritual undercurrent** — in deeper Thai cultural memory, ปล่อยของ carries a mystical weight. Something powerful being released. Not everyone will feel this layer consciously. The people who do are exactly our people.

The name is the product philosophy. Everything we build must honor all three meanings.

## THE FOUNDER'S TRUTH

Chairit Smittee is not a tech founder who researched a market. He is the market.

A successful professional whose hobby became a second education — collecting knives, vintage tools, Japanese denim, German dolls, objects that most people walk past and never see. To collect seriously you must become an insider, a researcher, a traveler. You must have money and patience and a tolerance for obsession.

He learned two things that nobody has built a platform around:

**Getting in is hard.** The best objects never appear in public markets. They move between people who know each other. To find them you need to be trusted, known, present. You need to be inside.

**Getting out is harder.** When a collector wants to sell, they face an impossible choice. Post on Facebook and invite scammers to steal your photos. List on Lazada where a 100-year-old German cuckoo clock has no context, no story, no dignity. Use a representative quietly and hope they know the right buyers. Or just keep it and tell yourself you'll deal with it later.

There is no dignified, safe, community-first place for serious collectors to exist. That is the gap. That is Ploikong.

## WHO WE SERVE — THREE CHARACTERS

### The Collector (every member starts here)
Successful. Curious. Slightly obsessive. Has more objects than space. Proud of their taste but private about their wealth. Would never post their collection on Facebook — too exposed, too many wrong people watching. Wants to connect with people who understand what they own and why it matters. May not want to sell anything. Their presence alone — their collection, their story, their knowledge — is valuable to the community.

**What they want:** To be seen by the right people. To find others like them. To know their collection is documented, valued, safe.

**What they fear:** Exposure. Scammers. Being misunderstood. Selling to someone who won't appreciate what they are receiving.

### The Buyer
Often the same person as the Collector in a different moment. Has specific taste. Has done research. Wants provenance, story, condition — not just a price. Will pay premium for trust and certainty. Hates the anxiety of last-second eBay bidding. Would enjoy a dignified auction experience if one existed.

**What they want:** Access to objects they cannot find anywhere else. Confidence in what they are buying. A way to participate that feels like the real thing — an auction room, not a countdown timer.

**What they fear:** Fakes. Overpaying. Buying something with hidden damage. Being outmaneuvered by someone with more information.

### The Broker (maximum 5 in Year 1, verified by Ploikong)
An expert in one or more categories. Known and trusted within collector communities. Acts as representative for buyers and sellers who want complete privacy. The human face of trust when trust is everything. Also the person who sells on behalf of estates, families who inherited collections and don't know what they have.

**What they want:** A legitimate, professional platform for their expertise. Tools that make their job easier. A way to serve clients who need complete discretion.

**What they fear:** Platform taking too much. Platform making them irrelevant. Platform not understanding that their relationships are their product.

## THE PLATFORM PHILOSOPHY

**Community before transaction.**

A new member joins Ploikong and has nothing to sell. This is not a problem. This is the beginning of the journey.

Their first week looks like this: They see other collectors' objects — not listings, but collections. They read stories written by people who understand what it took to find something. They follow a knife collector from Chiang Mai who knows things they want to know. They add three items to their watchlist. They feel, for the first time, that there is a place where their obsession is normal.

They may not buy or sell anything for a month. That is fine. When they do, they are ready. They trust the platform because the platform trusted them first — it let them belong before it asked them to transact.

**The three stages of a Ploikong member:**

1. **Curious** — browsing, following, watching. No pressure. Community only.
2. **Showing** — uploading their collection. Building their profile. Writing their first story. Becoming visible to the right people.
3. **Transacting** — when they are ready, on their terms. Fixed price, open offer, silent bid, or the Bid Room.

We never push anyone from stage 1 to stage 3. The community is the product. The transaction is the service.

## THE VAULT — our most underrated feature

Every serious collector needs this and nobody offers it.

A private, encrypted digital inventory of everything they own. Photo. Name. Year. Origin. Condition. Story. Estimated value. Purchase price. Who they bought it from.

Visible only to them. Always.

Why this matters:
- **Insurance** — "what do I own and what is it worth?" answered instantly
- **Succession** — when they die, their family inherits a documented, valued collection instead of a room full of mysterious objects
- **Selling when ready** — one click converts a Vault item to a listing. The story is already written.
- **Peace of mind** — collectors are anxious about their things. The Vault is the answer.

Future: Ploikong offers professional valuation services, estate sale facilitation, and family succession planning as premium features. The Vault is the gateway to all of them.

Private Vault items are invisible to everyone. When the owner chooses, they can make items visible to followers only, or make them public. This controls their exposure completely.

## THE BID ROOM — Khun Mod and the Virtual Auction

This has never been built. It needs to be built right.

**The problem with existing auction systems:**
eBay is anxiety. A countdown timer, last-second sniping, staring at a number hoping nobody jumps in. This is not how serious collectors want to buy important objects. This is not Christie's. This is not dignified.

**The Ploikong Bid Room:**

Up to 10 bidders in a virtual room. Each appears as a silhouette — no name, no face, no identity. Numbered: Bidder 1 through Bidder 10.

When someone bids, their silhouette raises a hand.

**Khun Mod** — the AI facilitator — announces in Thai first, English if a bidder responds in English. Fast-talking. Warm. Theatrical. Knows the object, knows the room, knows who has been quiet too long.

*"฿45,000 จาก Bidder 3! ยังมีอีก 6 ท่านในห้อง... ใครจะสู้ต่อ? อย่าปล่อยให้ไปง่ายๆ นะคะ"*

Visitors can watch. They cannot bid. The room has an atmosphere — you feel the tension of 10 people who all want the same thing.

**Identity rules in the Bid Room:**
- Bidders are silhouettes. Always.
- After the auction, the winner's identity is revealed to the seller only — never to other bidders.
- Members who want complete privacy use a Broker to bid on their behalf. The Broker's identity is visible. The member behind them is not — even to Ploikong.

**Toggle to Silent Mode:**
For sellers who prefer the eBay model — highest bid wins, no theater, no room. Same system, different experience. Seller chooses at listing time.

**Bid Room as social event:**
Upcoming auctions are announced in the feed. Members save the date. There is anticipation. The Bid Room is not just a transaction — it is an event that the community watches and talks about after.

## THE AI BROKER — Khun AI

Long-term vision. Not Phase 1.

A fully AI-operated broker available 24/7. Trained on Ploikong's rules, platform data, and collector knowledge. Handles:
- Valuation guidance ("based on similar items sold here, your piece is likely worth...")
- Listing assistance ("let me help you write the story of this object")
- Buyer matching ("three members on this platform have watched similar items")
- Private negotiation facilitation between buyer and seller

The AI Broker eliminates the legal complexity of human brokers — no commission dispute, no rogue agent, no privacy breach. The AI never reveals member identities. The AI follows platform rules without exception.

Members pay a small fee per AI Broker interaction. This is a revenue stream and a trust signal.

Human brokers (maximum 5, verified) remain for members who want human judgment and personal relationships. The AI Broker serves everyone else.

## CONTENT MODERATION — community rules and safety

This must be designed before any community feature goes live.

**Automated screening (at upload):**
- Image nudity detection before any photo becomes visible
- Keyword flagging in listing descriptions and stories
- New member posts held for 24-hour review period before public visibility

**Community reporting:**
- Every item, story, and profile has a report button
- Three reports from different members triggers automatic hold pending staff review
- Broker can flag any listing they believe is misrepresented

**Live and video content:**
- No unmoderated live streaming. All video content is pre-recorded and reviewed before publication.
- Video uploads screened for prohibited content before going live

**Prohibited content (permanent ban):**
- Fakes or misrepresented items presented as authentic
- Stolen goods (INTERPOL database check for high-value items flagged)
- Prohibited items by Thai law
- Sexual content of any kind
- Personal attacks or harassment within the community

**The consent framework:**
Every member signs a clear, Thai-law-compliant consent covering:
- Platform rules and consequences
- Escrow and payment terms
- Dispute resolution process
- AI Broker usage terms
- Data privacy (PDPA compliance)
- Broker liability limits

Members cannot transact until consent is signed. Consent is re-triggered when terms change. This is already built in the backend (legal.js pending).

## DESKTOP EXPERIENCE — the cinematic cube

The desktop is a different product from the mobile. It is an experience, not a utility.

**The concept:** A large interactive space — not a scrolling page. Items exist in space. The collector navigates, not scrolls. Think of a beautifully lit cabinet of curiosities that you can move through.

**Homepage hero:** Full viewport. Dark. An object — beautifully lit, slowly rotating or completely still. No clutter. One line of text. The feeling of walking into a room where something extraordinary is waiting.

**The cube / mosaic concept:** A grid of collected objects that has depth and dimension. Click into it and the world opens. Community, stories, collections. You must have something to show to fully participate — this is the mechanism that drives collection uploads.

**Navigation:** Minimal. Almost hidden. The objects are the navigation.

**Technology note:** This requires WebGL or advanced CSS 3D. This is a Phase 4 feature. Phase 3 desktop is clean, elegant, dark luxury — Playfair Display headlines, generous spacing, cinematic photography. The cube comes when we have the community to fill it.

## SISTER PLATFORMS

Ploikong is not a standalone project. It is the first and most complex layer of a collector-and-community infrastructure.

**Barrowme.com** — borrow instead of buy. C2C lending with insurance escrow. "I need a projector for one weekend." The platform holds a security deposit, both sides are protected, the object moves and comes back. Same trust engine as Ploikong. Community of people who believe in access over ownership.

**Aotop.com** — Thai local maker marketplace. B2B and B2C. Fills the gap between talented Thai producers (soap, fabric, food, craft) and global markets. Inspired by Alibaba but for Thai SMEs who are invisible internationally despite making world-class products. Government alignment opportunity. Cheap fee at scale becomes significant revenue.

All three platforms share:
- The same authentication and member trust system
- The same escrow and payment infrastructure  
- The same consent and legal framework
- The same moderation principles
- One operator (Janishammer Co., Ltd.)

## THE ARCHITECTURE VISION — Two Trees, One Forest

*This section explains why Ploikong was built the way it was built. Every future AI agent working on this ecosystem should read this before touching anything.*

### Ploikong is not a marketplace. It is the first proof of concept for a trust infrastructure platform.

The same engine that lets a collector sell a 1987 Japanese knife with dignity — with escrow, community, verified identity, and a story attached — is the same engine that will let a borrower pick up a camping tent from a smart locker, or let a Thai soap maker in Chiang Rai reach buyers in Riyadh, or let a collector pass their entire life's collection to their children with every item documented, valued, and ready to find its next owner.

Ploikong is the proof. What comes after is the platform.

---

### Tree 1 — The Ploikong Root (web-based trust and community)

```
Ploikong.com          — collectors meeting, showing, trading
        ↓ same backend engine, different community door
BorrowMe              — borrowers accessing things they only need once  
        ↓ same trust layer, different market
Aotop                 — Thai makers reaching global buyers
```

**What is shared across all three:**
- Member identity and trust system
- Consent framework (PDPA-compliant, lawyer-reviewed)
- Payment escrow (Omise → platform hold → release on confirmation)
- Community infrastructure (follow, story, watchlist, notification)
- AI capabilities (Khun Mod the auctioneer, AI Broker, content moderation)
- Cloudflare Workers backend — one architecture pattern, three deployments

**What is different in each:**
- The community (collectors vs borrowers vs makers)
- The transaction type (sale vs borrow-return vs wholesale inquiry)
- The fee structure (configured per platform in platform_config)
- The front door — the design, the name, the feeling of arrival

A buyer who joins Ploikong to buy a vintage knife — that same person might use BorrowMe to borrow a projector for a weekend presentation, and might discover on Aotop a soap maker from Lampang whose products they can't find anywhere else. One identity. Three doors. One trust infrastructure.

---

### Tree 2 — The Satu Root (physical IoT infrastructure)

```
Satu                  — ESP32 vending machine, temple donations, PromptPay
        ↓ same hardware, different firmware mode
AIoT Guide            — Claude-powered location kiosk, multilingual Q&A
        ↓ same pattern, different physical use
Smart Gate            — pay-per-access entry, privacy-first (motels, condos, events)
Smart Motel           — room access, time-based payment, automated checkout
        ↓ same pattern, scaled and licensed
IoT SaaS Platform     — white-label backend for any operator with ESP32 hardware
```

**What is shared across all Satu-tree deployments:**
- ESP32-S3 hardware platform (same supply chain, same production knowledge)
- Cloudflare Workers pull-model command queue (payment confirmed → device unlocks)
- Omise payment trigger (PromptPay → confirmed → hardware action)
- OTA firmware update system (update any device remotely via R2)
- PDPA framework (encrypted ID, 90-day auto-delete, court-order log)
- Heartbeat monitoring (any device offline → alert)

**The Satu Platform insight:** One Cloudflare Worker, one D1 database, unlimited tenants. Add `tenant_id` to every table, derive it from the API key on every request. Any business with ESP32 hardware can run on this backend. The software is built once. The revenue multiplies with each new operator.

---

### The Two Trees Together

These are not two separate businesses that happen to share an owner. They are two separate technology platforms that share:

- The same founder's judgment
- The same infrastructure knowledge (Cloudflare, Omise, PDPA, Claude API)
- The same relationship capital (temples, collectors, Thai maker communities, government networks)
- The same legal and consent framework
- The same AI agent operating model

**They can be sold separately.** The Ploikong Tree to a regional e-commerce or fintech acquirer — Grab, Sea Group, Ascend — who wants the trust layer and collector community. The Satu Tree to a property tech company, a hardware manufacturer, or a government smart city initiative who needs distributed IoT with payment at the edge.

**They can grow independently.** A bad season for collector markets does not affect smart gate deployments. A hardware supply problem does not affect the web platform. The two-tree model is the domino protection.

**Together they make the founder unkillable as a one-person operation** — because no single market failure, no single technology shift, can take both trees down at the same time.

## THE PERSONAL OPERATING SYSTEM — The Ecosystem Behind Everything

Above the two trees, there is a third layer that nobody else can see or copy.

Chairit Smittee is building a **personal knowledge ecosystem** — a living, linked intelligence system where every idea, every financial movement, every project decision, every product vision document, every collection item, and every lesson learned is connected to everything else.

Not a dashboard. Not a notes app. A **founder's brain in digital form.**

Built with:
- Personal financial log (income, expense, movement across businesses)
- Blog and idea capture (thoughts that become products)
- Project planning layer (the plans that become prompts that become code)
- Collection database (personal items that will feed Ploikong inventory)
- Obsidian-style linking — every node connects to related nodes

**Why this matters for AI agents:**

Any AI agent — Chat, CC, Claude Design, or a future specialized agent — dropped into this ecosystem does not need to be re-briefed. The context is already there. The history is already there. The decisions and their reasons are already there.

This is the **asset memory model** — a system where the knowledge compounds over time rather than being lost between sessions. Every lesson learned becomes a permanent node. Every product decision links to the conversation that produced it. Every financial movement connects to the project it funded.

When this ecosystem is complete, a new AI agent can read it and understand not just the current task but:
- Why the business exists
- What decisions were made and why
- What has been tried and failed
- What the founder's values and non-negotiables are
- Where the vision is going

**This document — product_vision.md — is one node in that ecosystem.** It links to master_seed.md (the technical memory), to lessons_learned.md (the mistake memory), to the financial log (the resource memory), and to the collection database (the product memory).

The ecosystem is the moat that no competitor can replicate — because it is built from lived experience, real decisions, and years of compounding knowledge. It is not data. It is wisdom in a format that AI can read and act on.
